Where can I find my model and serial number?

Look for the identification plate on the machine frame. Its location varies by model; your operator manual can point you to the exact spot.

How do I find the right replacement filter?

Use the model and serial information when contacting an authorized parts dealer. This helps match the correct filter to your equipment.

What should I include in a service request?

Share your model, serial number, a short description of the issue, and the best way to reach you. Never operate equipment that feels unsafe.

How do I check the status of my request?

Use the request number and the same email address you submitted with the request in the tracker below.
